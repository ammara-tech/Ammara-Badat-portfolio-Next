I wrote my capstone case study for recruiters and hiring managers at South African software companies who want to hire junior developers. A case study is a short story about one project: the problem, my role, the tools I used (called the stack), screenshots and what I would do next. The biggest thing I cut was detail: my presentation about the clinic platform is about nine minutes long, so smaller features like the doctor pop-up, keyboard shortcuts and live notifications became one line each, and the screenshots and a five-minute demo video show the rest. I also took Python off my skills because I don't have a public project that proves it yet, and now every skill on my Skills page links to proof, like a file on GitHub, a certificate or a live website. My About page now explains in about 30 seconds who I am and that I'm looking for a junior full stack job in Gauteng, and that I can start immediately.
