Milestone 5 reflection
Live URL: https://ammara-badat-portfolio-next.vercel.app
Lighthouse scores (mobile, Home page): Performance [96], Accessibility [100], Best practices [100], SEO [100]

The biggest thing Lighthouse found was that my Home page was slow on phones: Performance was only 74, even though Accessibility and SEO were already 100. My animations kept the heading and intro text invisible for almost two seconds, so Lighthouse counted the page as “not loaded yet” (this is called Largest Contentful Paint, or LCP, the time until the biggest piece of content shows). I changed the animations so the text slides in but is visible straight away, and I made the search box and image viewer load only after the page appears, which cut the JavaScript that runs at the start. My Performance score went from 74 to 88 to 96. I also gave every page its own title, description and share picture, and added a sitemap and robots file so Google can find all my pages.
